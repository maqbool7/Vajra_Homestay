Welcome to Vajra

Heritage, Stillness and Comfort

Vajra is a warm and quiet place away from busy life. Surrounded by beautiful mountains, everything here is made with love—from our stone pathways to fresh home-cooked meals.

Enjoy hot single-origin coffee on your balcony in the morning, and look up at the clear Milky Way sky with the naked eye at night.

Eco-conscious

Solar-assisted, no single-use plastics, organic grounds.

Farm to table

Local recipes made with ingredients harvested daily.

Private and serene

Gated property with restricted entry for your peace.

Personal care

Warm hosts who look after your wellbeing.
